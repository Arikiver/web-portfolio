DESCRIPTION
  A cozy stylized 3D travel simulator inspired by late-night trip plans, long
  drives, beautiful landscapes, and the feeling of exploring the world with
  friends.

  About the game:
  "Chalo Yaar!" is a cozy first-person travel experience where you explore
  peaceful environments, drive your campervan across beautiful landscapes,
  capture moments through photography, and simply enjoy the atmosphere of the
  journey.

  The game was inspired by a very relatable feeling: that one late-night
  conversation where everyone suddenly decides, "Chal yaar, Goa chalte hain."
  Plans get made. Everyone gets excited. And then life happens. Schedules
  clash, budgets fail, parents say no, and the trip never happens.

  This project was created to capture that feeling digitally — the freedom of
  the road, the calmness of travel, and the warmth of shared adventures.

  Gameplay experience:
  This is not a fast-paced action game or a hardcore survival experience.
  "Chalo Yaar!" is designed to feel relaxing, immersive, and atmospheric. The
  focus is on enjoying the journey itself — driving through nature, exploring
  new places, listening to ambient sounds, and experiencing the calm feeling
  of being on the road. Whether you're walking through forests, driving during
  sunset, or stopping somewhere just to admire the environment, the game aims
  to recreate the peaceful feeling of travel and freedom.

FEATURES
  - Drive your own campervan
  - Explore large stylized natural environments
  - Wildlife photography system
  - Interactive inventory and travel systems
  - Atmospheric volumetric fog
  - Custom stylized water shaders
  - Dynamic foliage and wind simulation
  - Immersive region-based ambient audio
  - Cozy exploration-focused gameplay
  - Dynamic cinematic environments

TECH / TOOLS
  - Unity Engine (URP)
  - C#
  - HLSL shader development
  - Blender

DEVELOPER NOTES
  Developed as a passion-driven project focused on atmosphere, immersion,
  rendering, and exploration systems. A huge amount of work went into the
  visuals, gameplay systems, shaders, audio atmosphere, and overall feel.
  Closing line: "Pack your bags. Start the engine. Hit the road."

INSTALL INSTRUCTIONS
  1. Download and extract the ZIP file
  2. Open the extracted folder
  3. Run ChaloYaar.exe
  4. Wait for the game to load
  Notes: Windows only; first launch may take a few seconds; headphones
  recommended; optimized for keyboard and mouse.
